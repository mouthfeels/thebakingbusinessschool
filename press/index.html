About the book

Skilled in the kitchen. Untrained in the business behind it.

Most baking education teaches technique: recipes, decoration, presentation. Almost none of it teaches the business decisions that decide whether a bakery survives — pricing, positioning, systems, marketing and long-term sustainability.

The Baking Business Strategy is written for baking entrepreneurs who already know how to bake, and now need to know how to run a business. It sets out the strategic thinking behind pricing for profit, building repeatable systems, positioning a brand, and scaling without losing quality or sanity.
